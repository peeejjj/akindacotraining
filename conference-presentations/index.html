Guest lectures and invited presentations

Alongside conference work we offer a range of guest lectures into higher education organisations. Our educators hold current university teaching appointments alongside clinical caseloads, so a guest lecture brings live casework into the room rather than a set of slides about it. Contact us to discuss topics and availability.
