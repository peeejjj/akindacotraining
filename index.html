A values-driven training experience

What our training actually gives you

Our educators hold both extensive academic teaching experience and current clinical caseloads. That combination shapes every day we run.

Workbooks that hold the theory

You leave with a concrete written understanding of the fundamental content, not a slide deck you never open again.

Reflection on the self of the therapist

Structured activities on the self of the therapist, on management, and on the wider system you work inside.

Reflexive work with children and families

In-depth exploration of reflexive practice, so you can stay curious when a family brings complexity into the room.

Experiential and creative practice

Case studies and hands-on practice of experiential and creative ways of working, because complexity is rarely solved by talking about it.

Techniques you can use on Monday

Practical techniques to implement with clients, plus informative practice exercises to rehearse them before you do.

Delivered in-house if you prefer

Every course can be brought to your service, school or centre and shaped around the team you already have.

What you get from all of us together

AkindaCo is a multidisciplinary mental health and allied health practice, not a training company that also sees the odd client. That distinction shapes what a training day feels like. When someone in the room asks the hard question, the honest one about the family that will not shift, the answer comes from a caseload rather than a textbook.

It also means the disciplines talk to each other. Systemic family therapy, art psychotherapy, positive behaviour support, DBT and early childhood practice sit in the same building, in the same supervision, on the same board and management team. A course built here carries all of that, which is why our family therapy training takes attachment and behaviour seriously, and why our art therapy training is grounded in psychotherapeutic theory rather than activities.

Our values are accessibility, collaboration and justice. We strengthen children's development by supporting the entire network of carers, educators and community members around them, and we work the same way with the professionals we train.

Supervision, placements and guest lectures

Beyond the open courses

Training is one part of what we do with the profession. The rest is supervision, growing the next workforce, and teaching inside universities.

Clinical supervision

Shane Fotheringham, Rana Kökçinar, Paula Gillespie-Fotheringham and Kate Maitland all offer clinical supervision. Between them that covers systemic and family therapy, art psychotherapy, positive behaviour support, child and adolescent work, perinatal and infant mental health, and complex adult practice, so you can be matched to a supervisor who actually works in your area rather than adjacent to it.

Alongside individual supervision we provide professional development and tailored systemic training and development for whole teams. Get in touch to talk about what you need.

Student clinical placements

We take students on clinical placement from the University of Adelaide, IKON Institute, and some interstate providers including the University of Melbourne.

We expect students to stay for the whole year. Placement here is not a rotation you pass through. Students specialise, either in working with children and young people, or in working with adults, and they are supported to go deep in that area across the full year.

From next year we will also offer placements for professionals interested in positive behaviour support, alongside our PBS practice.

To apply, email your CV and an expression of interest to hello@akindaco.com.

Guest lectures

We offer a range of guest lectures into higher education organisations. Our educators hold current university teaching appointments alongside their clinical work, so a guest lecture from AkindaCo brings live casework into the room rather than a set of slides about it. Contact us to discuss topics and availability for your program.

Questions

Common questions about our training

Where can I do family therapy training in Adelaide?

AkindaCo runs Family Therapy Foundations, a two-day PACFA-endorsed intensive, at 183 Tynte St, North Adelaide. The next intake is 17–18 September 2026. It is open to counsellors, psychologists, social workers, occupational therapists, school wellbeing leaders and community-sector practitioners, and no prior family therapy training is needed. Full details are here.

Where can I do art therapy training in Adelaide?

AkindaCo runs Art Therapy Foundations, a two-day introduction delivered at locations across Adelaide. It covers the theoretical, psychological and ethical foundations of art therapy alongside practical skill development. Dates for the next intake are being confirmed. Full details and interest registration are here.

Do your courses count as continuing professional development?

Family Therapy Foundations is PACFA-endorsed CPD. Our courses are also routinely used for CPD by members of AASW, ANZACATA, AAFT and PTPA. If your association requires a specific format of evidence, tell us before the day and we will provide it.

Do I need prior training to attend?

No. Family Therapy Foundations and Art Therapy Foundations are both foundation-level courses and assume no prior training in that modality. They are designed for professionals who already work with people and want a new way of working.

Can you deliver training at our organisation?

Yes. Every course can be delivered in-house and shaped around your team, and our Preschool Boost programs are only delivered in-service at your centre. Contact the team to talk about what your service needs.

Who teaches the courses?

Practising clinicians from the AkindaCo team, led by Rana Kökçinar, Senior Psychotherapist, Family Therapist and Education Lead. AkindaCo's founder, Paula Gillespie-Fotheringham, is an Adjunct Lecturer at the University of Adelaide. Meet the full team.

Do you offer supervision and consultancy as well?

Yes. Alongside open courses we provide clinical supervision, professional development, and tailored systemic training and development for teams. Get in touch to discuss what you need.

Does AkindaCo take student placements?

Yes. AkindaCo partners with a number of higher education institutions to support emerging psychotherapists, art therapists and family therapists through placement and beyond.
